About Us

We build AI that solves real problems

OneSphere Labs is an Australian AI product studio. We build specific products for specific problems in healthcare, compliance, parenting, and pet care.

Our Approach

Every product we build starts with a real problem we've seen firsthand. Australian doctors drowning in paperwork. Compliance teams dreading audits. Parents struggling without support. Pet owners missing signals their animals are sending.

We build vertical AI — technology designed for a specific industry, a specific workflow, and a specific regulatory context. Not horizontal platforms that try to do everything for everyone.

Founded in 2024 and based in Australia, we operate with Australian Privacy Principles (APP) compliance, Sydney-based data residency, and a deep understanding of the regulatory frameworks our customers work within.

Privacy First

APP compliant. Sydney data residency. On-device processing where possible. Your data is never used for AI training.

Regulation Aware

Built for Australian regulatory frameworks — RACGP, TGA, PBS, ASQA, NDIS, aged care standards.

Patent-Pending Innovation

Our dual-camera, on-device AI analysis technology (Attune) has a patent filed — real invention, not just an API wrapper.
