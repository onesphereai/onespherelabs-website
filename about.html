About Us

We build AI that solves real problems

OneSphere Labs is an Australian AI product studio. We build specific products for specific problems in healthcare, compliance, parenting, and pet care.

Founded by a Stanford-trained technologist and a 25-year enterprise transformation veteran, OneSphere Labs combines deep architectural rigor with lived operator experience. We do not just guess what industries need. We run the clinics and operations that face these challenges daily. By leveraging advanced AI frameworks, we build production-ready platforms in weeks, not months or years.
