Petsense

Your pet's health, decoded. AI-powered wellness monitoring from your phone.

Download the App → Visit petsense.com.au

Your pet cannot tell you when something is wrong. By the time symptoms are visible, conditions may already be advanced. Petsense uses your phone's camera to detect early signs of illness through behavioural analysis, gait tracking, and coat condition monitoring. It learns your pet's baseline and alerts you when something changes, before it becomes a vet emergency.
