The Problem

Pets communicate through behaviour, posture, and sound. Most owners miss the signals, not because they do not care, but because nobody ever taught them how to read their dog or cat. Anxiety, discomfort, and stress often go unnoticed until they become bigger problems.

The Solution

Petsense uses your phone camera and AI to analyse your pet's behaviour in real time. Point, observe, and understand. Whether your dog is anxious or your cat is overstimulated, Petsense tells you what is happening and what to do.

Key Features

Real-Time Behaviour Analysis

Point your camera at your dog or cat and get an instant read on their emotional state and behaviour. Petsense identifies signals that most owners would not notice.

Dog and Cat Support

Built to understand the distinct body language of both dogs and cats. Whether it is tail position, ear movement, posture, or vocalisation, Petsense reads the full picture.

Behaviour History

Track your pet's behaviour patterns over time. Spot trends, identify recurring triggers, and understand what environments or situations affect your pet most.

Actionable Guidance

Every analysis comes with clear, practical guidance on how to respond. Not just what your pet is feeling, but what you can do about it right now.

Breed Awareness

Petsense accounts for breed-specific behaviours and tendencies, so the guidance you receive is relevant to your specific animal, not just dogs or cats in general.
