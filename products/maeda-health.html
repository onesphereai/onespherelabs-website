Meet Your Clinical AI Team

Six specialised agents work together in the background, orchestrating complex clinical tasks that used to consume your day.

Patient Data Agent

Autonomously searches, retrieves, and structures data from multiple sources. Pulls full submission histories, reviews past consultations, and generates comprehensive clinical summaries — ready before you ask.

Clinical Decision Support Agent

Cross-references patient profiles against current guidelines. Runs eligibility and contraindication checks. Calculates BMI, eGFR, and cardiovascular risk scores. Generates personalised monitoring timelines based on treatment protocols.

Clinical Knowledge Agent

Searches RACGP, ANZOS guidelines, TGA approval statuses, and PBS listings in real time. Checks drug interactions across entire medication lists. Returns cited, current answers grounded in Australian evidence.

Practice Management Agent

Generates operational dashboards on demand — no manual report running. Tracks submission statistics, pending review queues, team workload distribution, and compiles comprehensive billing summaries across your clinic.

Report Generation Agent

Gathers data from multiple sources to produce publication-ready documents. Drafts specialist referral letters with embedded clinical reasoning. Creates longitudinal treatment progress reports and auto-fills uploaded templates.

Document Workflow Agent

Analyses the structure of any uploaded form — checkboxes, date fields, text areas — and intelligently maps patient data to every field. Renders completed, high-fidelity PDFs without manual intervention.

From Patient Arrival to Signed Document

See how Maeda's agents orchestrate your entire consultation workflow.

1. Patient Intake

AI-powered intake forms with conditional logic capture patient information before the appointment. Background agents process responses, pull relevant history, and prepare clinical context.

2. Correspondence Processed

Incoming scanned mail, faxes, and digital correspondence are automatically classified by type and urgency, then routed to the correct inbox. Relevant clinical data is extracted and linked to patient records.

3. Live Transcription

AI Scribe records and transcribes the consultation in real time across 5 languages — Mandarin, Hindi, Arabic, Vietnamese, and Greek. Smart Dictation captures clinical nuance.

4. Notes & Billing Generated

Structured SOAP notes are generated automatically. MBS billing codes are suggested based on the full consultation context — services rendered, time spent, complexity assessed.

5. AI Assistant Available Throughout

At any point, ask your AI Clinical Assistant a question. Your agents search guidelines, check interactions, calculate risks, and return cited answers — all within the context of the patient in front of you.

6. Forms Auto-Completed

Select from 14+ Australian government and clinical forms — WorkCover, Centrelink, DVA, Mental Health Treatment Plans. The Document Workflow Agent maps patient data to every field and renders a completed PDF.

7. Review, Sign, Export

Everything is prepared by your agents. Review the generated content, refine with inline editing, apply your digital signature, and export finalised records. The consultation is complete.
