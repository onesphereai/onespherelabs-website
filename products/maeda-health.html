The Problem

Australian doctors spend hours every day on documentation, forms, billing, and correspondence. That time comes directly at the expense of patients. Most AI tools only solve one piece of the puzzle.

The Solution

Maeda covers the entire consultation workflow from first patient contact to final signed document. One platform replaces the scribe, the form filler, the billing tool, and the clinical reference. Built specifically for Australia.

Key Features

AI Clinical Assistant

An always-on conversational AI inside the doctor portal. Ask clinical questions, explore differential diagnoses, check drug interactions, and get evidence-based answers grounded in Australian guidelines (RACGP, TGA, PBS) with source citations, all within the context of the patient in front of you.

AI Medical Scribe

Real-time transcription and instant SOAP note generation during consultations. Supports five languages including Mandarin, Hindi, Arabic, Vietnamese, and Greek. Smart Dictation mode for notes recorded outside of consultations.

MBS Billing Suggestions

AI recommends the correct Medicare billing codes based on consultation content and duration. Schedule fees displayed instantly. Prevents missed items and reduces billing errors.

Post-Consultation Forms

Automatically completes 14+ Australian government and clinical forms after every consultation: WorkCover certificates, Centrelink documents, Mental Health Treatment Plans, referral letters, and more.

Specialist Referral Search

Finds local specialists and auto-generates referral letters on the spot, drawing directly from consultation notes.

Smart Patient Forms

Drag-and-drop form builder with conditional logic for patient screening. AI Form Assistant generates complete forms from plain English descriptions. Outcomes feed directly into the doctor's workspace.

Correspondence Management

Reads and classifies inbound scanned mail from hospitals, assigns urgency levels, and routes documents to the correct doctor's inbox automatically.

Clinical Knowledge Base

Build a personal or shared knowledge base from trusted guidelines and research papers. Ask questions and receive cited answers tailored to your specialty.
