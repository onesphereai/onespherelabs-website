The Administrative Burden Is Unsustainable

Australian GPs spend up to half their working hours on documentation, form-filling, correspondence management, and billing administration. Every minute spent on paperwork is a minute taken from patient care — and the administrative load is only growing as compliance requirements increase.

One Platform for Your Entire Clinical Workflow

Maeda replaces fragmented tools and manual processes with a single, interconnected platform. Real-time clinical documentation, automated government forms, intelligent billing suggestions, correspondence triage, and an AI clinical assistant — all purpose-built for Australian healthcare and accessible from one workspace.

From Screening to Sign-Off in Seven Steps

Maeda supports the full consultation lifecycle. Every step is connected — patient data flows forward automatically, so nothing is duplicated and nothing is missed.

1. Screen

Send AI-powered intake forms to patients for eligibility checks and pre-consultation triage.

2. Prepare

Patient responses and AI-classified correspondence are loaded into your consultation workspace automatically.

3. Record

Begin the consultation and record in any supported language with real-time transcription.

4. Generate

AI produces structured SOAP notes, suggests MBS billing codes, and identifies required referrals.

5. Ask the AI

Query the AI Clinical Assistant to check guidelines, drug interactions, or patient history mid-consultation.

6. Document

Generate referral letters, auto-fill government forms such as WorkCover and Centrelink, and prepare correspondence.

7. Review and Export

Refine documents with AI-assisted editing, apply digital signatures, and export as PDFs.

The Platform

Seven core capabilities, one connected workspace. Each pillar is powerful on its own and seamlessly integrated with the others.

AI Clinical Assistant

A conversational AI with specialised agents that work across your clinical data. Ask questions in natural language and get structured, actionable answers — from patient history lookups to clinical decision support.

  • Patient Data Agent — Retrieves and structures full submission histories and consultation records instantly.
  • Clinical Decision Support Agent — Cross-references patient profiles against guidelines, checks eligibility, calculates BMI and eGFR, provides dosing guidance.
  • Clinical Knowledge Agent — Searches Australian clinical databases including RACGP, TGA, and PBS for treatment protocols and drug interactions.
  • Practice Management Agent — Generates operational dashboards on demand covering team workload, billing summaries, and practice metrics.

AI Medical Scribe and Multi-Language Consultations

The foundation of every consultation. Maeda listens, transcribes in real time, and generates structured SOAP notes as you speak — in English or across supported languages.

  • Real-time transcription with structured SOAP notes streamed during the consultation.
  • Multi-language support for Mandarin, Hindi, Arabic, Vietnamese, and Greek — AI transcribes, translates in real time, and generates English clinical notes.
  • Smart Dictation mode for adding notes outside of active consultations.

Documents and Post-Consultation Forms

Complex clinical and government paperwork, generated automatically from your consultation notes. Review, refine, sign, and send — without re-entering a single data point.

  • Auto-fills 14+ Australian forms including WorkCover (all states), Centrelink SU415, DVA D904, NDIS evidence forms, GP Mental Health Treatment Plans (MBS 2700, 2701, 2715, 2717).
  • Specialist referral letters drafted from consultation context.
  • AI Edit Bar for refining tone, length, and content with natural language prompts before digital signature.

MBS Billing Suggestions

After your SOAP notes are generated, Maeda analyses the clinical content, consultation duration, telehealth mode, and billing type to recommend the correct MBS item numbers. Each suggestion includes the clinical rationale, schedule fee, and Medicare benefit amount. You simply accept or reject.

Correspondence Management

Scanned paper mail from hospitals, specialists, and insurers — handled automatically. Maeda uses OCR to extract text from scanned PDFs, classifies each document by type and urgency, and routes it to the correct doctor's inbox.

  • Document classification: referral, lab result, specialist report, insurance correspondence.
  • Urgency tagging: routine, urgent, or critical.
  • Automatic routing to the correct clinician's inbox.
  • Critical reports flagged immediately — no manual sorting required.

AI-Powered Insight

Build a custom clinical knowledge base from sources you trust. Upload PDFs, web URLs, and research papers — including RACGP guidelines and local health pathways — then ask clinical questions and receive answers synthesised exclusively from your uploaded sources, with exact inline citations. No hallucinated references. No uncited claims.

Smart Patient Forms

A form builder for screening, intake, and risk assessment — with conditional logic, scoring rules, and AI generation.

  • Eligibility and information forms with conditional logic and scoring rules for auto-calculating risk assessments.
  • AI Form Assistant: describe the form you need in plain English and Maeda generates the fields, logic, and decision rules.
  • Patient responses are summarised by AI and flow directly into the doctor's consultation workspace.
