For Parents of Neurodivergent Children

Attune

A private AI coach that understands your child.

The Problem

Parents of neurodivergent children face difficult moments every day, often without support nearby. Generic advice does not account for each child's specific diagnosis. And most parents are left guessing.

The Solution

Attune gives parents a real-time AI parenting coach that actually knows their child. Point your phone during a difficult moment and get instant, diagnosis-aware guidance. Everything happens on your device. Nothing leaves your phone.

Key Features

Live Mode

Both cameras work at once. The front camera reads the parent's stress level. The back camera reads the child's behaviour. Within moments, Attune tells you what is happening and what to do, tailored to your child.

Record Mode

Record a 60-second clip of a difficult moment. Attune produces a full breakdown: what happened, when your child was calm versus stressed, what you did well, and what to try next time.

Ask Attune

Type any question and get an answer shaped around your child's specific diagnosis and profile. Not generic advice. Guidance that actually fits your family.

Child Profile System

Every child's profile includes their diagnosis, sensory sensitivities, and known triggers. The AI adjusts its interpretation accordingly. Hand-flapping in an autistic child is understood as self-soothing, not a crisis.

On-Device Processing

All video and audio analysis happens on the device itself. No footage is uploaded to cloud servers. No sensitive data about your child sits on someone else's system. Ever.

Neuro-Affirming Language

Attune treats neurodivergent behaviours as differences to understand, not problems to fix. Every response is written to support and empower parents without judgment.
