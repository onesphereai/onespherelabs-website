Vigility

Compliance that runs itself. Audit-ready, always.

Visit vigility.com.au Request a demo

The Problem

Compliance teams at RTOs, NDIS providers, and aged care facilities spend enormous time manually tracking regulations, chasing evidence, managing credentials, and dreading audits. A single missed requirement can mean penalties, failed audits, or loss of registration.

The Solution

Vigility turns compliance from a reactive, stressful process into an always-on, automated one. It knows what you need to comply with, tracks how you are meeting those requirements, and generates a complete audit pack at the click of a button.

Key Features

Regulatory Standards Library

An up-to-date library of obligations relevant to your industry, whether you are an RTO, NDIS provider, or aged care facility. Always know exactly what is required of you.

Controls Mapping

Define your organisational controls (policies, procedures, processes) and map them directly to the regulations they satisfy. See your coverage and gaps at a glance.

Automated Evidence Classification

Upload compliance documents and AI automatically categorises them, tracks expiry dates, and links them to the right controls. No more manual filing.

Staff Credential Tracking

Tracks trainer qualifications, vocational competencies, and unit-level teaching authorisations. Know who is authorised to deliver what, and when credentials are expiring, before it becomes a problem.

One-Click Audit Pack Generation

AI analyses your evidence against selected standards and produces a structured audit report with findings. What used to take days of preparation now takes minutes.

CAPA Tracking

When gaps or non-conformances are found, the platform creates findings and tracks corrective actions with owners, timelines, and status. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Live Compliance Score

A real-time risk score tells you where you stand at any moment, with historical snapshots so you can see your compliance posture improving over time.
